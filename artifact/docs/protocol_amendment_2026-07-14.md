# Protocol Amendment: Cohort and Endpoint Hierarchy

**Recorded:** 2026-07-14, after the initial audit outcomes were known and before
submission. This document is an amendment, not a claim of preregistration.

## Why this amendment exists

The initial contract required a resolvable official repository for inclusion.
That criterion conditions the audit on artifact availability, which is related
to the outcomes under study. A post-freeze search also recovered plausible
official links for candidates recorded as unresolved. Separately, the original
E3 endpoint combined as-documented and repaired executions even though repair
eligibility and semantic equivalence involve auditor judgment.

## Changes

1. The 35 candidates are the screening frame. The 26 releases resolved before
   outcomes were known remain the frozen primary audit cohort.
2. The completed search resolves 30/35 repositories. The four post-freeze
   resolutions are fully coded and executed under the same rules, but are
   reported as an expansion sensitivity rather than retrospectively entering
   the primary cohort. R2 and E3 are not population prevalence estimates.
3. E3 first-pass (as documented) is the least discretionary execution estimate.
   E3 after repair is a secondary sensitivity analysis. Both are reported.
4. R2 is LLM-assisted descriptive coding because the model-panel nominal
   reliability gate was not met; it is not human-coded ground truth.
5. The words "pre-specified" and "frozen contract" replace "preregistered"
   unless an immutable external registration can be cited.

## Frozen results retained and extension completed

No primary labels or execution outcomes were changed by this amendment. The
four re-screened releases were frozen, coded, and smoke-tested on 2026-07-16;
their evidence and results are released in `rescreened_release_coding.json`,
`expanded_sensitivity_outcomes.csv`, and `rescreened_smoke_results.json`.
